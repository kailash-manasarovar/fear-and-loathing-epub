you’ll hear me hailing you from inside the open grave, and you’ll realize how we’ve always been together.

I am the clear consciousness-core of your being, the same in ecstasy as in self-hating fatigue.

That night, when you escape the fear of snakebite and all irritation with the ants, you’ll hear my familiar voice, see the candle being lit, smell the incense, the surprise meal fixed by the lover inside all your other lovers.

This heart-tumult is my signal to you igniting in the tomb.

So don’t fuss with the shroud and the graveyard road dust.

Those get ripped open and washed away in the music of our finally meeting.

And don’t look for me in a human shape. I am inside your looking. No room for form with love this strong.

Beat the drum and let the poets speak. This is a day of purification for those who are already mature and initiated into what love is.

No need to wait until we die! There’s more to want here than money and being famous and bites of roasted meat.
